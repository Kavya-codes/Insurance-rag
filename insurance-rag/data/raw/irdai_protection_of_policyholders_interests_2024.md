# IRDAI Protection of Policyholders' Interests Regulations (2024)

## 1. Customer Information Sheet (CIS)
Under the IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests, Operations and Allied Matters of Insurers) Regulations, 2024, every insurer must provide a concise Customer Information Sheet (CIS) along with the policy document in simple, accessible language.

The CIS must clearly summarize the policy name, sum insured, coverage highlights, waiting periods, sub-limits, co-payment requirements, deductibles, permanent exclusions, claim submission process, free look period, portability options, and grievance redressal contact details. Insurers must obtain acknowledgment of the CIS from the policyholder where applicable.

## 2. Proposal Form and Underwriting Turnaround Time
Every insurer must process a proposal for insurance and communicate its underwriting decision in writing within 15 days from the date of receipt of the proposal or any additional information requested. If the insurer calls for medical reports or further details, the request must be communicated promptly.

A copy of the completed proposal form—whether physical or electronic—must be furnished to the policyholder free of charge within 30 days of acceptance of the proposal. Any material information disclosed in the proposal form forms the basis of the insurance contract.

## 3. Policy Cancellation and Premium Refund
A policyholder may cancel a retail health or general insurance policy at any time by giving 7 days' written notice to the insurer. For policies with a tenure of more than 1 year where no claim has been reported, the insurer must refund the proportionate premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis for multi-year policies or as per the specified refund scale.

An insurer may cancel a policy only on grounds of established fraud, misrepresentation, or non-disclosure of material facts, and must provide at least 15 days' written notice to the policyholder before cancellation takes effect.

## 4. Grievance Redressal Mechanism and Bima Bharosa
Every insurer must establish an accessible Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO) at its corporate and regional offices and integrate with IRDAI's Integrated Grievance Management System (IGMS), known as Bima Bharosa. Upon receiving a complaint, the insurer must send a written acknowledgment to the complainant within 3 working days.

The insurer must resolve the grievance and send a final response within 14 days from the date of receipt of the complaint. If the insurer rejects the complaint or fails to respond within 30 days, the policyholder may escalate the matter to the Insurance Ombudsman.

## 5. Insurance Ombudsman Jurisdiction
Under the Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended), an individual policyholder, legal heir, or nominee may file a complaint with the Insurance Ombudsman free of cost—no lawyer is required. The complaint must be filed within 1 year from the date of rejection of the representation by the insurer or after 30 days of no response.

The Insurance Ombudsman has pecuniary jurisdiction to award compensation up to Rs. 50 lakh (Rupees Fifty Lakhs), including any ex-gratia payment or interest. Complaints may cover partial or total repudiation of claims, delay in claim settlement, premium disputes, misrepresentation of policy terms, or non-issuance of policy documents.

## 6. Nomination and Beneficial Nominee Rules
Under Section 39 of the Insurance Act, 1938, the holder of a life insurance policy on their own life may nominate one or more persons to whom policy moneys shall be payable in the event of death. Where the policyholder nominates their parents, spouse, or children (or any of them), such nominees are treated as beneficial nominees, meaning they are entitled to receive the policy proceeds beneficially to the exclusion of other legal heirs, unless expressly directed otherwise.

For general and health insurance policies, insurers must capture nominee details at the proposal stage so that any admissible claim amounts payable after the death of the insured are settled smoothly to the designated nominee or legal heir.
