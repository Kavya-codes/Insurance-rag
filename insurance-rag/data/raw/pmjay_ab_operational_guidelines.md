# Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY) Operational Guidelines

## 1. Scheme Overview and Coverage Limit
Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY) is India's flagship publicly funded health assurance scheme implemented by the National Health Authority (NHA). It provides cashless health coverage of up to Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs) per eligible family per policy year on a family floater basis for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.

The Rs. 5,00,000 cover can be utilized by one or more members of the beneficiary family in a single policy year across empanelled public and private hospitals.

## 2. Beneficiary Eligibility and Senior Citizen Expansion
Eligibility for AB PM-JAY was originally determined using the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 database based on deprivation criteria for rural households and defined occupational categories for urban households, along with families covered under the erstwhile Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY).

There is no cap on family size, age, or gender under AB PM-JAY, ensuring all eligible family members including newborns and elderly dependents are covered. Additionally, under the senior citizen expansion (Ayushman Vaya Vandana Card), all citizens aged 70 years and above—irrespective of socio-economic status or income—are entitled to Rs. 5,00,000 annual top-up coverage, shared among eligible 70+ members in a family.

## 3. Pre-Existing Diseases and Waiting Period
Under AB PM-JAY, all pre-existing diseases and medical conditions are covered from Day 1 of enrollment. There is zero waiting period for pre-existing ailments, chronic conditions, or specific surgeries under the scheme.

## 4. Pre-Hospitalization and Post-Hospitalization Benefits
Every admitted inpatient package under AB PM-JAY covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization expenses (including diagnostics and medicines prior to admission) and up to 15 days of post-hospitalization expenses (including follow-up medicines and consultations after discharge) as part of the defined Health Benefit Package (HBP) rate.

## 5. Health Benefit Packages, Zero Co-Payment, and National Portability
Treatments are reimbursed to empanelled healthcare providers (EHCPs) based on standardized Health Benefit Package (HBP) rates covering medical, surgical, daycare, and follow-up procedures. Beneficiaries pay zero co-payment, zero deductible, and zero user fees; empanelled hospitals are strictly prohibited from balance billing or charging beneficiaries extra money for covered package procedures.

AB PM-JAY provides seamless national portability: an eligible beneficiary from one state can avail cashless hospitalization at any empanelled hospital in any other participating state or Union Territory across India by verifying their identity via Aadhaar e-KYC and their Ayushman Card.

## 6. Beneficiary Verification and Grievance Redressal
Beneficiary identity is verified at the hospital helpdesk (Pradhan Mantri Arogya Mitra) using the Beneficiary Identification System (BIS) through Aadhaar-based biometric, OTP, or iris authentication to generate the Ayushman Card.

Beneficiaries experiencing denial of cashless care, overcharging, or service quality issues can lodge complaints via the 24x7 toll-free national helpline 14555 or the Centralized Grievance Redressal Management System (CGRMS). Grievances are handled across a three-tier structure—District Grievance Redressal Nodal Officer (DGRNO), State Grievance Redressal Officer (SGRO), and National Health Authority (NHA)—with a standard resolution timeline of 15 days.
