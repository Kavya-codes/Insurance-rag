STANDARD HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY WORDINGS (AROGYA SANJEEVANI MODEL)

1. OPERATIVE CLAUSE AND SUM INSURED OPTIONS
This Policy is a standard individual and family floater health insurance contract issued in India. The available Sum Insured options range from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 10,00,000 in multiples of Rs. 50,000. The policy covers reasonable and customary medical expenses incurred by the Insured Person during the Policy Period for medically necessary hospitalization in India.

2. IN-PATIENT HOSPITALIZATION AND ROOM RENT SUB-LIMITS
In-patient hospitalization requires admission as an inpatient for a minimum period of 24 consecutive hours, except for listed Day Care procedures where 24-hour hospitalization is not clinically required due to technological advancement.

Room Rent, Boarding, and Nursing Expenses as provided by the Hospital are payable up to 2% of the Sum Insured per day, subject to a maximum of Rs. 5,000 per day. Intensive Care Unit (ICU) or Intensive Cardiac Care Unit (ICCU) charges are payable up to 5% of the Sum Insured per day, subject to a maximum of Rs. 10,000 per day.

If the Insured Person occupies a room category higher than the eligible room rent limit, proportionate deduction will apply to all associated medical expenses (such as surgeon fees, consultant charges, and diagnostic tests), except the cost of medicines and consumables billed at MRP.

3. PRE-HOSPITALIZATION AND POST-HOSPITALIZATION EXPENSES
Medical expenses incurred immediately prior to the date of hospitalization are covered up to 30 days preceding admission, provided such expenses are directly related to the ailment for which hospitalization was subsequently required.

Medical expenses incurred immediately after discharge from the hospital are covered up to 60 days from the date of discharge, provided they relate directly to the same illness or injury for which the inpatient claim was admitted.

4. AMBULANCE AND CATARACT SUB-LIMITS
Road ambulance charges incurred for transporting the Insured Person to the nearest hospital in an emergency are covered up to a maximum of Rs. 2,000 per hospitalization.

Expenses incurred for treatment of Cataract are subject to a sub-limit of up to 25% of the Sum Insured or Rs. 40,000 per eye, whichever is lower, in each policy year.

5. CUMULATIVE BONUS AND CO-PAYMENT
The Sum Insured shall be increased by 5% in respect of each claim-free policy year, subject to a maximum cumulative accumulation of 50% of the basic Sum Insured. If a claim is made in any particular year, the accrued Cumulative Bonus will be reduced by 5% at the subsequent renewal, without reducing the base Sum Insured.

Every admissible claim under this standard policy is subject to a mandatory co-payment of 5% to be borne by the Insured Person, irrespective of the age of the insured at entry or renewal.

6. INITIAL WAITING PERIOD AND STANDARD PERMANENT EXCLUSIONS
An initial waiting period of 30 days applies from the first policy inception date for any illness other than an accidental injury. Any injury resulting solely from an accident is covered from Day 1.

Standard Permanent Exclusions include:
(a) Cosmetic or plastic surgery unless necessitated due to an accident, burn, or cancer reconstruction;
(b) Participation in hazardous or adventure sports as a professional or without safety compliance;
(c) Intentional self-injury, suicide, or attempted suicide;
(d) War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, or nuclear radiation perils;
(e) Treatment for alcoholism, drug or substance abuse, or any addictive condition;
(f) Unproven or experimental treatments not recognized by standard medical protocols;
(g) Maternity expenses, infertility treatment, and routine dental or optical expenses unless arising from an accident requiring hospitalization.
